## Campaign creatures

Only the campaign places these (owner 8), with the same columns, from their `.chr` records (C). Not generated:
`mods/pw/rules/wild.yaml` has their rules by hand. Speeds in the rules are px per tick × 16 (two world ticks per
original tick); the aliens' shot (JW2_12 `Alien.wpn`) has no projectile speed in its record, so the mod gives it the
arrows' 18 px.
