# Tyrano

Generated by `research/tools/game_tables.py` from the `.chr` type records and the `JW2_10` tables; field meanings
in [rules.md](rules.md) and [chr.md](../formats/chr.md). Times are ticks, with seconds at the default speed (71 ms).
Range and sight are in pixels; speed is the average east walk step in pixels per tick; "Delay" is
`.chr` 0x13cc (attack delay, D). "Needs" for upgrade levels counts only flagged levels.

Tech tree: TyranoNest and Nest need nothing; EggNest → LandNest, SkyNest, ThrowNest, UpgradeNest, Ascomoid;
LandNest + UpgradeNest → LandNisdos; SkyNest + UpgradeNest → SkyNisdos. Tyranos needs every other building.
Merged units (TwinVelocis, TwinRhamphos, Mutant) cost nothing; they come from merging (see
[rules.md](rules.md#merging-tyrano)). Ground mutation armour level 5 costs 100 in the data, probably a typo for
1,000.
