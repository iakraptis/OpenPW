# Elf

Generated by `research/tools/game_tables.py` from the `.chr` type records and the `JW2_10` tables; field meanings
in [rules.md](rules.md) and [chr.md](../formats/chr.md). Times are ticks, with seconds at the default speed (71 ms).
Range and sight are in pixels; speed is the average east walk step in pixels per tick; "Delay" is
`.chr` 0x13cc (attack delay, D). "Needs" for upgrade levels counts only flagged levels.

Tech tree: Heaven and ElfTree need nothing; Heaven → RedHall, BlueHall; both → ManaHall, ElfLibrary; ManaHall →
SkyHall, ManaLibrary; SkyHall → SkyLibrary; ElfTree → Bubble. The AngelElf needs every other building. Elf buildings
are conjured (no construction stages). Spells and their levels: [rules.md](rules.md#spells-jw2_11). Data fixes in
the mod: Study Divine being's Weapon Secret level 3 takes 3,360 ticks (1,200 in the data, every other level 3 of its
kind 3,360), and record 47, which raises armour, is named "Study Divine being's Armor Secret" (the data repeats the
weapon one's name).

The Bubble tower's recorded attack value (25) does not reduce hit points in the original: its bubble traps the target
instead. The mod applies a 36-tick immobilizing condition: the weapon's 36 impact frames (`JW2_12` `Bubble.wpn`,
0x20c), one per tick as weapon frames advance (C, [effect.md](../formats/effect.md)); that the hold lasts as long as
the animation is deduced (D). It deals 1 damage per hit as a design choice.

## Look

Elf buildings are conjured: being built, the finished building fades in while the Elf Build sparks play over it;
destroyed, it fades out under the same sparks (`PwConjured`). The Wizard starts its attack animation at a bush, holds
the middle casting pose while gathering, then keeps casting toward the berry as it flies to the Heaven. It watches the
berry until it arrives before starting the next gathering cycle. During gathering the Wizard channels a lightning beam
to the bush; during building it stays beside the site and channels a beam into it instead of entering it. The gathering
beam stops while the berry flies. The Wizard's blue spell glows are drawn over it while gathering and building; the
Elf Build sparks play over the unfinished building. The Bubble Tower's bubble animation is drawn over trapped units
at quarter scale.

## AI

The bots cast the Elf spells with `PwSpellBotModule` (rules/ai.yaml). They choose by what a spell does:

| Spell | When |
|---|---|
| Thunder bolt | on the enemy unit it kills, otherwise the most expensive one |
| Drop stone, Jump portal | where 3 or more enemy units stand within the radius |
| Meteor (kills the ManaSpread) | where 6 or more enemy units stand |
| Shield | on an army unit under fire, without a shield |
| Mass temper | over 3 or more unshielded army units, in a fight |
| Fake | on the most expensive unit nearby, in a fight |
| Resurrect | where 2 or more units fell lately |
| Teleport | the Ranger flees home below 35% health, with enemies near |

Cure needs no orders: an idle Unicorn heals the unit Cure was last cast on, otherwise the most hurt ally in reach,
2 HP for 1 mana a tick (`PwSpell.AutoHeal`); the AI leaves it to that. The research a spell needs (Study Fake,
Study Meteor) is started once the bot has the caster. The unarmed casters (Unicorn, ManaSpread) are trained by weight,
stay out of the attack squads, and follow the bot's main group.
